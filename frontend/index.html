Why We Picked circulant_n9_o1-2

We did not select Processor D as a "lucky winner". We mapped the entire Pareto trade-off curve of Number of Edges (chip cost proxy) vs. Compiled CX Gate Count:

  • Minimizing Circuit Depth: While circulant_n9_o1-3 saves 3 CX gates, our choice achieves a lower two-qubit depth (87 vs 90), executing within tighter qubit coherence times ($T_1, T_2$).
  • Vertex-Transitive Symmetry: The chordal circulant has an automorphism group of order 18. Every qubit is topologically equivalent, eliminating routing sensitivity to initial mapping.
  • Chip Area & Yield: Restricting physical qubits to exactly 9 (instead of 10–12) avoids unnecessary transmon frequency collisions while saturating the allowed degree of 4.
